# Aetheria Systems: 2026 Employee Benefits Overview

## 1. Health and Wellness

All full-time employees are eligible for the **Platinum Tier Health Plan** through NexusCare.

- **Mental Health:** 12 sessions of subsidized therapy per year.
- **Gym Reimbursement:** Up to $100/month for fitness-related expenses.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

Aetheria operates on a "Flexible Results" policy. While there is no hard cap, we mandate a **minimum of 15 days** taken per calendar year to prevent burnout.

## 3. Remote Work and Stipends

Employees may work from anywhere within ±4 hours of the UTC-8 timezone. New hires receive a one-time **$1,500 Home Office Stipend** to be used within the first 90 days of employment.
